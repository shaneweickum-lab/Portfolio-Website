## What it is

A focused tool for authors: drop in a manuscript, get back a narrated
audiobook-style MP3. It's deliberately its own standalone, installable PWA
rather than a page bolted onto this site — a separate app with its own
domain, so it can grow independently of the portfolio around it.

## How it works

- **Frontend** — a small Next.js PWA: drag-and-drop upload, a live progress
  view while the document is narrated, then an inline player and MP3
  download.
- **Backend** — a Python FastAPI service that parses the document, runs it
  through [Piper](https://github.com/OHF-voice/piper1-gpl) (a fast, local
  neural TTS engine) paragraph by paragraph with natural pauses between
  them, and encodes the result to MP3.
- Conversion runs as an async background job with status polling, since a
  full manuscript can take real time to narrate — not something you want to
  hold a request open for.

## Formats

Free today (no paywall exists yet, but the format gating is already wired
in for later): **.txt** and **.md**. Also supported right now: **.docx**,
via `python-docx` — currently free as well, and slated to move behind a
paid tier once one exists.

## What's next

PDF support (straightforward to add), a real paid tier, and possibly more
Piper voices to choose from.
