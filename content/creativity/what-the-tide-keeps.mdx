The tide does not keep grudges.
It keeps *addresses* —
door numbers painted on brick
long after the brick has forgotten
what a door was for.

Ask it for a name
and it will give you salt.
Ask it for a name twice
and it will give you a diver's silhouette,
going down anyway.

Nothing down there remembers being lost.
Only the ones still breathing do that.
