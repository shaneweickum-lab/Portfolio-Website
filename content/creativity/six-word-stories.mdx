A recurring exercise: say something true in exactly six words. No cheating with hyphens.

- Map redrawn. Nobody told the river.
- She kept the key. Sold the house.
- The agent apologized. It meant it.
- Found the letter after the funeral.
- We rebuilt the city, not ourselves.
- The model was right. I wasn't.
